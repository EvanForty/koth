How the crown is won

Every coin on the pump.fun bonding curve qualifies automatically, not just new launches. Only coins at least halfway to graduation can wear the crown. Scores update every two seconds over a rolling 10-minute window, and a challenger has to beat the king by 5% to take the hill. When the king graduates, the next coin in line is crowned.
